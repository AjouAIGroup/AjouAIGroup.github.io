Ajou AI Group Researchers at EMNLP 2026

Research teams led by Professors Kyung-Ah Sohn, Jongbin Ryu, and Hyunsouk Cho presented a total of six papers at EMNLP 2026 (Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing), one of the world's leading academic conferences in natural language processing.

  • From left: Donggeon Lee (student), JuHeon Ha (student), Byounghan Lee (student), Soyeon Hong (student), Hyunsouk Cho (professor), Seungmin Oh (student), Dooyeon Na (student), and EunJu Shin (student).

  • From left: Jieun Han (researcher), Byounghan Lee (student), EunJu Shin (student), Seungmin Oh (student), Dooyeon Na (student), Donggeon Lee (student), JuHeon Ha (student), and Soyeon Hong (student).

  • Presentation of the paper "Empathy Is Steerable but Multi-Axial: Mechanism Geometry and Persona Effects in LLMs," co-authored by Professor Kyung-Ah Sohn and students JuHeon Ha, Byounghan Lee, and Yunseo Choi.

  • Presentation of the paper "Train Overcomplete, Deploy Compact: Scaling Recovery Capacity for Structured LLM Pruning," co-authored by Professor Jongbin Ryu and students Seungmin Oh and Donggeon Lee.

  • Presentation of the paper "Layer-wise Curriculum Learning for Efficient LLM Compression," co-authored by Professor Jongbin Ryu and students Donggeon Lee, Dooyeon Na, and Seungmin Oh.

  • Presentation of the paper "Layer-wise Curriculum Learning for Efficient LLM Compression," co-authored by Professor Jongbin Ryu and students Donggeon Lee, Dooyeon Na, and Seungmin Oh.

  • Presentation of the paper "Colla-Q: Toward Collaborative Experts in MoE Quantization via Minimax Precision Balancing," co-authored by Professor Jongbin Ryu and student EunJu Shin.

  • Presentation of the paper "ASSAY: Reconciling Stability and Validity in Reference-Free Scientific LLM Judges," co-authored by Professor Hyunsouk Cho and students Soyeon Hong and Heegyu Kim.

  • Presentation of the paper "Filter by Quality, Stop by Difficulty: Confidence-Aware Self-Consistency with Adaptive Early Stopping," co-authored by Professor Hyunsouk Cho and students Jooyeong Na and Wansik Jo.
